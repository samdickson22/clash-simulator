# Fable — Round 1 proposal (independent, no consensus claimed)

Base: main `95feeb0e` plus the coordinator's uncommitted repairs. Everything below was checked against source, receipts and datasets on the Mac mini on 2026-09-28. Labels: **[F]** verified fact, **[H]** hypothesis, **[D]** decision Sam or the coordinator must make.

## 1. What the evidence actually says

- **[F] Hardware.** M4 Pro, 12 cores, 24 GB, MPS, torch 2.10. Disk is 8.1 GiB free (below the 11 GiB noted in the handoff). Load average ~14 from other threads' TLA+ jobs in `~/formal`; no Clasher process, emulator off.
- **[F] Throughput.** Python scalar engine: ~920 decisions/s single-process random play, ~1,650 row-ticks/s at batch 128 (`reports/perf_baseline_m4.json`, `python_vs_simple_gym_*`). Historical full recurrent PPO loop: 255–360 learner decisions/s. The tensor "simple gym" on MPS is *slower* than Python on this machine (84–851 row-ticks/s depending on capacity); it only wins on CUDA (`reports/python_vs_simple_gym_capacity_study_20260828.md`). Native reference collection costs ~8–10 min per configuration (capture median 133 s, native branches median 333 s, scalar 16 s; v7 job receipts), so a v7-shaped 128-configuration campaign is ~20 emulator-hours.
- **[F] The only measured actor is weak and passive.** The promoted Hog 2.6 champion (`reports/hog26_current_champion_20260827.json`, 1.75M params, structured memory 64, hazard hierarchy, semantic+mechanics slot adapters replacing the base head, 167k-sample imitation fit then 14 PPO updates / 57k transitions) scored 15/24 vs random, 22/48 vs strategy bots, 8/24 on matched baselines, and in the paired strategy matrix waited on 93% of playable decisions and lost 2/2 to `balanced`. No later actor exists; September work was outcome forecasting (6,144 games, 2.47M rows) and simulator fidelity.
- **[F] Model code.** `rl/model.py` `ClasherPolicy`: entity transformer encoder (d=128, 4 heads), LSTM/GRU/structured memory, card-conditioned cross-attention decoder over 576 tiles, action-type head (4 slots+wait+ability), privileged critic encoder, opponent-hand/elixir auxiliary heads, hazard and continuous-time event hierarchies (`deterministic_hierarchy in {slot, play-gate, hazard, event}`), plus ~15 optional repair/prototype adapters. `PolicyConfig` defaults have the adapters off; the champion had them on.
- **[F] Contracts.** Public actor observation: 128 entities × 32 features, 18 globals, 5 visible card slots (hand+next), optional public history (4), seen-card slots (8), visible `entity_levels` (contract v3), confidence channels, real-play feature subset (17/32 entity features camera-derivable). `PublicActionMaskBuilder` (v5) masks from public data only. `StructuredPublicStateTracker` gives exact revealed-card/cycle bookkeeping without parameters. `live_inference_contract.py` fails closed on privileged payloads.
- **[F] Simulator scope.** 171 card definitions; simple-gym support artifact covers 66 public cards / 33 meta decks; the *native-calibrated* public roster is 16 cards (`PUBLIC_REFERENCE_CARDS`: Archers, Cannon, DarkPrince, Fireball, Giant, Goblins, HogRider, IceGolem, IceSpirit, Knight, Log, Musketeer, Prince, Skeletons, Tesla, Zap). Hog 2.6 is entirely inside that roster. Card levels: `_create_card_stats_from_data(level=11)` and `stat_scaling.scale_stat` support any level, but `PlayerState`/`apply_deck_to_player`/`SelfPlayBattleEnv` have no per-player level plumbing. Mixed-level play is therefore *not* currently trainable.
- **[F] Opponents.** `strategy_bots.py` (6 public-info scripted styles, PFSP table), `public_scripted_opponent.py` (16-card roster, 3 styles), random, frozen checkpoints; `train_recurrent.py` league mode; `deck_curriculum.py` (2,652 procedural decks split 1,758/312/582 with four held-out archetypes).
- **[F] Human data.** IL_Replay: action logs only, modern forms dominate (level 16: 71,839 vs level 11: 7,495 sides in the 5k shard), every placement form-unknown, no state. Camera-derived corpora exist in the structured contract: KataCR 347 episodes / 383,670 rows / 17,331 plays (4.5%), TV-Royale-trained 794 episodes / 596,905 rows / 27,067 plays. Privileged-oracle teacher corpora exist (`teacher_broad_v1_merged`: 176 episodes). Bulk YouTube extraction is blocked on HUD variant recognition and a content-policy question.
- **[F] Gates.** `docs/design/CALIBRATION_PROTOCOL.md` blocks all fitting, PPO, search and teacher distillation until fresh public-state and counterfactual-ranking acceptance. v7 (64 families, 0 bad, 95% bound <5%, 81 HP regret) failed at 12/128 on a movement clamp, now repaired; 13 opened captures × 64 branches pass under the original criteria as development evidence only.
- **[F] Test coverage gap.** Only two test files touch `ppo_update`/`compute_gae`; recurrent burn-in, terminal-vs-truncated bootstrap and Python-backend log-prob recomputation have no direct regression tests.

**Diagnosis.** The binding constraint is not simulator fidelity and not the missing forecaster. It is that no training loop has yet produced basic adaptive competence, and the attempts that exist were seeded from wait-dominated camera corpora, trained for tens of PPO updates, carried an accreted adapter stack, and were then frozen behind a 20-hour native gate that failed on a clamp. Sam's new tolerance (treat dynamics deltas like balance changes; hunt systematic ranking errors and sim-only exploits under reacting play) means the gate should be re-shaped around the *policy's own decisions*, not around fixed-family trajectory agreement.

## 2. Three materially different combinations

| | A. Fidelity-first replay imitation → league PPO (current design) | B. Model-free self-play PPO from scratch on the tensor gym | C. Privileged-teacher seed → reacting-opponent recurrent PPO on the scalar engine, human data as prior/diagnostic (**recommended**) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Architecture | Entity transformer + structured belief; replay-reconstructed state | Same encoder or a cheap 32×18 raster CNN; LSTM; no teacher | Entity transformer + LSTM(256) + deterministic cycle tracker, event-time hierarchy, privileged critic, adapters off |
| Supervision | IL_Replay reconstructed through the sim | Terminal reward only | Privileged fixed-depth oracle labels on student-visited states (DAgger), then PPO; camera corpora as low-weight placement/timing prior and human-likeness eval |
| Blocking dependency | Modern-form reconstruction (41 evo + 14 hero variants outside certified scope); fresh 64-family native acceptance (~20 emulator-hours per attempt) | Throughput: needs 10^5–10^6 games; MPS gym is slower than Python here; no paid compute allocated; known inert/spam failure mode (FirstLight's 296/306 wins vs near-inactive opponents) | Level plumbing, one fresh config, event-loss wiring check, ~5 emulator-hours for a bounded prospective gate |
| Time to first complete-game policy | Months | Weeks with a GPU; not executable locally | Days (local) |
| Ceiling | Highest strategic prior if reconstruction works | High with scale | Bounded by oracle quality and sim fidelity, but improvable by search/league later |
| Verdict | Right long-run data source, wrong next step | Right scaling path, wrong first step | Most informative next experiment per hour |

An architecture-only alternative, the public action-value set ranker (`structured_action_value.py`) fit on counterfactual branches, predicts the incumbent's outcomes but yields no policy without a search loop; keep it as a later leaf evaluator, not the actor.

## 3. Recommendation: C, specified

**Actor information boundary.** Actor inputs are exactly the `StructuredObservationBuilder` public arrays with `canonical_perspective=True`, `canonical_lane_globals=True`, `card_semantics_version=4`, `public_history_slots=4`, `public_seen_card_slots=8`, `public_entity_levels=True`, masked by `PublicActionMaskBuilder` only. Critic uses the existing privileged encoder. Opponent hand/elixir remain auxiliary *prediction* heads (already present), never inputs. Phase 1 trains on exact simulator observations (`actor_observation_domain="simulator-exact"`); degradation via `degrade_simulator_public_observation` is a later robustness phase, not mixed into the competence pilot.

**Representation and memory.** One fresh `PolicyConfig` preset, call it `council-v1`: d_model 128, 4 heads, actor 4 layers, critic 2, `memory_kind="lstm"`, memory 256, `structured_deterministic_resource_enabled=True` (exact cycle tracker), every repair/prototype/slot-choice adapter off, `hierarchical_mode_gate_enabled=False`. Capacity changes only after held-out action error shows the representation, not data or update bugs, is limiting (per `docs/design/PIPELINE_DESIGN.md`).

**Actions and timing.** Keep the 2,306-action space (slot×576 tiles + wait + ability); the EVENT_AUDIT half-tile Tesla anchors already round-trip through `building_anchor`. Use `deterministic_hierarchy="event"` (continuous-time rate, `event_policy.py`) so the play rate is cadence-invariant, with the categorical `slot` hierarchy as the bounded control arm. Decision interval 10 ticks for the pilot, with a 5-vs-10 cadence sensitivity arm **[H3]** before any broad run.

**Level and dynamics handling.** Operationalize "players adapt to balance changes" as declared nuisance families: (i) card and tower levels sampled per episode from {10, 11, 12} for both seats, *visible* through `entity_levels`; (ii) a hidden ±5% HP/damage jitter on two random cards per side per episode. Evaluate at fixed level 11 with jitter off, and on native. Ranges are frozen before evaluation. This requires the missing level plumbing (§5).

**Data and initialization.** Scope for the pilot is the 16 calibrated cards: Hog 2.6 as the fixed deployment slice plus 64 procedural 8-card decks drawn from the roster (the same draw family v7 used), so decks, mechanics and native reference all agree. Initialization is a DAgger loop with the privileged `FixedDepthThompsonOracle` (`oracle_planner.py`, `train_dagger_oracle.py`, `dagger_behavior.py`): 3 rounds, target 50k teacher-labeled decisions on student-visited states against the scripted pool, fit with `imitation.fit_imitation_corpus` (hierarchical masked loss). Camera corpora enter only as a low-coefficient `CausalDecisionRehearsal` placement/timing prior and as a human-likeness diagnostic set. IL_Replay stays read-only; deck composition sampling uses `datasets/real_common_decks_compatible_2026-08-11.json` (8 decks, 40 cards, only 1 inside the 16-card roster) as an out-of-scope stress slice, never relabeled forms.

**Opponent curriculum.** Fixed balanced schedule first, exactly as `configs/hog26_simple_competence_stage1_seed1192201.txt` does: uniform-legal random, the six `StrategyBot` styles, `PublicScriptedOpponent` balanced/pressure/defense, frozen DAgger init and later checkpoints. Pin the mix per collection segment, log episode-level opponent identity. PFSP (`allocate_pfsp_slots`) only after a reliable improvement baseline; exploiters later.

**Learning algorithm.** Synchronous recurrent PPO in `train_recurrent.py` via `ParallelRolloutCollector` (12 CPU actors) with the MPS learner. Reward: terminal ±1 plus `objective-v1` potential shaping with `reward_shaping_gamma` matched to PPO gamma; `elixir_leak_penalty_scale=0` (it is non-potential); keep and report the −0.01 illegal-action term. KL anchor to the DAgger init with a decaying coefficient. Candidate settings: 48 envs, 64 rollout steps, 2 epochs, sequence batch 8, lr 1e-4. Pass `verify_rl_training_stability.py` bounds every 10 updates.

**Evaluation.** `eval.py` with paired seats/seeds, 96 games per opponent type, learner deck sampled from the pilot pool, opponents from train decks and from the held-out archetype split. Metrics already emitted: score rate, `noop_when_playable`, `defensive_action_rate_when_threatened`, `defense_event_success_rate`, accepted-action rate, crown difference. Exploit screens: win rate vs a no-op opponent must not explain wins; repeated-action loop detector; top-k most frequent decision patterns replayed on native under reacting continuation.

## 4. Prospective two-tier readiness gate

Neither tier touches v7, its ledger, or its exposures. Both are declared before collection with fixed roots, seeds, criteria and missing-root-equals-failure.

- **Tier A (before promotable fitting).** Fresh independent 16-card roots with the existing fixed public controllers: 8 public-state reference roots (`public_reference_checks.py`, both seats) plus 30 counterfactual-ranking roots (`compare_reacting_public_branches.py`, 30-tick stride, wait/±1 tile/1-tick delay). Criteria: 0 strict outcome-reversal families for the selected action, 81 HP regret budget retained, 95% one-sided bound on bad-family rate below 10% (30 roots, zero bad), no clear regression vs recorded baseline. Cost ≈ 30 × 8 min ≈ 4–5 emulator-hours. Prior evidence for passing is the repaired 13 × 64 development recheck **[H5]**.
- **Tier B (before PPO continuation past the milestone, any search, and any promotion).** *Policy-conditioned* ranking: sample 30 decision roots from the frozen candidate's own scalar games, stratified by phase/deck/seat; branch {policy top-2, wait, ±1 tile} on native and scalar with reacting continuation; same criteria plus the exploit screens. This is where Sam's standard bites: it tests whether the sim teaches wrong *decisions*, not whether trajectories match.
- **Protocol revision [D].** Allow bounded *development* fitting on the scalar engine (checkpoints labeled non-promotable, ≤ 4 wall-hours per run) for loop debugging while Tier A runs in parallel; anything used as a teacher, opponent lineage, or competence claim requires Tier A first. This is a change to `docs/design/CALIBRATION_PROTOCOL.md`, stated openly, not a waiver.

## 5. First bounded local milestone (M1, "adaptive competence pilot")

Engineering prerequisites (no training):
1. Level plumbing: `PlayerState` card/tower levels, `apply_ordered_deck_to_player(levels=...)`, `BattleState.deploy_card` passing `level`, `SelfPlayBattleEnv` sampling, `entity_levels` emitted. Balance-jitter family in `balance.py` overrides.
2. `council-v1` config preset; verify the `event` hierarchy path in `imitation.py`/`train_recurrent.py` losses end to end (the model wires it; the trainers reference only `deterministic_hierarchy`).
3. PPO math regression tests: GAE terminal vs truncated bootstrap, recurrent burn-in equals uninterrupted inference, Python-backend log-prob recomputation (exists for the simple backend only).
4. Throughput measurement at the real shapes: `scripts/perf/benchmark_stationary_rollout.py` and `benchmark_oracle_clone.py` on the 16-card pool, interval 10, 12 actors; record decisions/s and oracle decisions/s **[H4: ≥250 learner decisions/s]**.
5. Policy-conditioned root sampler for Tier B (extend `select_reacting_public_root.py`/`prepare_prospective_branch.py`).

Then, contingent on Tier A and the emulator decision:
6. DAgger init: 3 rounds, 50k teacher decisions, ~2–4 CPU-hours (to be measured).
7. PPO: 200 updates (~1.2M transitions, ~1–2 h at 300/s) as a smoke, then 1,000 updates (~6–10 h overnight) with level randomization as one arm and cadence 5 as another.
8. Evaluation block, Tier B.

**Success criteria for M1 (frozen now):** ≥70% score vs random, ≥55% mean vs strategy bots with no opponent below 40%, `defensive_action_rate_when_threatened` ≥0.5, `noop_when_playable` ≤0.85, ≥45% on held-out-archetype opponent decks, Tier B zero reversal families, and PPO beats the frozen DAgger init at equal evaluation conditions.

**Continue/pivot rules.** PPO improves vs bots but Tier B shows systematic reversals → repair those exact mechanics, not general fidelity. PPO fails to beat the DAgger init after 1,000 updates with stability audits clean → investigate reward/exploration, then capacity (raster-CNN and d=192 ablations). Throughput below 150 decisions/s → the bottleneck is compute; only then propose a CUDA budget. Oracle too slow for 50k labels → fall back to scripted-bot behavior cloning as the seed.

**Compute.** M1 is entirely local: ~20 CPU/MPS wall-hours, ~5 emulator-hours (Tier A) + ~4 (Tier B), <2 GB of checkpoints (7 MB each; cap retained checkpoints). Recheck disk before every phase; the current 8.1 GiB is thin, and the machine is currently loaded by unrelated jobs [D: scheduling]. No paid compute now. The capacity study shows an A6000 simple-gym trainer at ~400 transitions/s is only ~1.3× this Mac's Python loop, so a GPU buys little until the learner is the bottleneck; the more valuable paid resource later is CPU-heavy actor pods, or a GH200 for YouTube extraction.

## 7. Principal uncertainties

**[H1]** A privileged-oracle DAgger seed produces an active, defending policy within ~50k labels (Boney et al. and the existing teacher corpora support this; the champion lineage never tested it cleanly). **[H2]** Level randomization improves native ranking consistency without costing more than 5 points at level 11. **[H3]** Cadence invariance under the event hierarchy. **[H4]** Local throughput ≥250 learner decisions/s. **[H5]** Tier A passes on fresh roots.

**[D]** Emulator restart for a bounded Tier A. **[D]** Adopt the two-tier protocol revision. **[D]** 16-card pilot roster now, expansion by calibrated bundles later, versus the 66-card simple-gym roster. **[D]** Machine scheduling against the other threads' jobs.

Human-level play remains the goal. M1 establishes only whether this stack can produce adaptive competence at all, which is the question nothing in the repository has yet answered.
