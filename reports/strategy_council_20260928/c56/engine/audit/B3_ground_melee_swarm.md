# B3 audit (ground melee / swarm / kamikaze) - read-only code+probe audit, 2026-10-03
Two consequential defects. **Berserker never deals damage**: its damage is stored in `attackSequenceList`, which the engine does not read, and its `Range` is missing, so it probes as `dmg 0, rng 0.0`. **Fire Spirit is not kamikaze**: it survives and fires every 300 ms, and one spirit killed a level-11 Knight in 9 hits. Golem, Wall Breakers, Royal Hogs, Mini PEKKA, Skeleton Army and Goblin Gang match native data at level 11. Smaller gaps: masses derived from collision radius for 5 characters, the missing 100 ms stagger between the two Rascal girls, and when Royal Ghost becomes visible.
