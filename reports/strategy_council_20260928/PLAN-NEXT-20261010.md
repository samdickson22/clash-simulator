## 0. Bottom line

1. **Main path: (b) make search anytime, then parallel. Not distillation.** E1's deadline W lost because of
   *ordering*, not raw cost. In `e1/planner.py`, deadline W runs the balanced coarse scan over **every** play
   before any candidate gets a complete score, and WAIT is scored **last**. A cutoff therefore leaves nothing
   complete, and 86% of decisions fall to v1, which baseline search beats about 58%. Two fixes:
   - score WAIT/WAIT10 (deduplicated) and the timed waits **first**, so a cutoff defaults to W's most common choice
     (65% of teacher roots) instead of v1;
   - run the remaining work on 4 GIL-release threads.

   This is CPU-only and about 3 h of build; the study takes about 2 h of wall time.
2. **ExIt r1 failed mainly on targets, not passivity.** I sampled 3,079 teacher roots from the packed corpus on 04
   (read-only):
   - the median gap between the top two root scores is **0.003** (p90 0.009);
   - at the frozen **T=0.1**, the softmax target puts a median of **9.6%** on the best action. The targets were
     close to uniform over candidates.

   Students therefore learned the candidate-set prior. At teacher roots their hard agreement is 0.555, *below* the
   all-WAIT constant (0.654). Free-running, they play **more** often than v1 (6.0% vs 4.3% of polls), so the problem
   is mistimed plays, not passivity. Class reweighting (rare-play ×4) was the wrong fix.

   r2 = **sharpened/argmax targets on the existing 38.7 M-row corpus**, no new labels needed. These fits fill the
   idle GPUs cheaply (~3 GPU-h per arm).
3. **(c) Hybrid (student top-k → W scores only those): not now.** Top-8 recall of teacher plays is 0.30 for
   S-teacher and 0.08 for S-human (≈v1). Restricting W to those proposals would discard most of W's chosen plays.
   Revisit once a student reaches ≥0.6 top-8 recall.

## 1. Does 4-thread W fit 200 ms? (estimate, to be measured in K)

Fixed-state speedup from 4 threads (w-confirm, symmetric W): p50 1.74×, **p95 2.70×**. The live four-root replay
gave 3.32× at p50.

| In-game W-screen8 distribution, 1 core | p50 / p95 / p99 ms | ÷2.7 (4 thr) | Decisions fully scored ≤192 ms, 1 thr → 4 thr |
|---|---|---|---|
| vs baseline search (w-screen8) | 177 / 330 / 460 | 66 / 122 / 170 | ~62% → **~99%** |
| vs v1, human proxy (E1 bW) | 331 / 626 / 857 | 122–190 / 232 / 318 | ~25% → **~60–70%** |

- **Against search-like states, the deadline problem largely disappears with 4 threads. Against human-like states
  it does not.** About a third of decisions still truncate, so anytime ordering is required on top of threads.
- The coarse scan is ~75% of the work (≈N+25 rollouts, with N≈70 plays vs v1). Running it at horizon 80 and
  refining at 160 gives ~1.6× more. Combined with 4 threads, that puts the v1-state p95 at an estimated ~145 ms.
- The Mac's per-core speed and the core contention from perception are unknown. That remains the E4 question.
