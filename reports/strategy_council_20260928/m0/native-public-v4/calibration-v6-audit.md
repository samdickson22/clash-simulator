# Scoped native public calibration audit

The new receipt audits saved data from the completed v6 development repetition. It does not modify that run's historical `public_calibration_established=false` result fields.

The audit covers 1,406 saved native decision frames and 2,812 owner packets: two repetitions of `opened-forward0-root90`, immediate play, balanced/pressure, ticks 90–3600 at five-tick intervals. These are repeated observations from one development family and condition, not independent acceptance families or a population sample.

All public packet hashes reproduce exactly from raw ordinary/rich/level evidence, declared vocabulary/ruleset and recorded public control history. Independent reference checks validate identity, ownership, kind, integer coordinate round trips, body/Crown health fractions, own HUD card slots and next card, own elixir and body/tower levels. The audit also validates public v4 shapes, dtypes, missingness and actor serialization; reconstructs public legal masks; checks recorded choices against those masks; and exhaustively round-trips every legal tile center through native integer coordinates. It observes 14 sparse-hand owner packets without shifting cards.

Known own card identities are preserved, while unmeasured own-card levels remain zero with confidence zero. The receipt lists the actual observed entity identities/effects per job; unobserved mechanics receive no calibration claim. Missing clock, motion, unsupported status/timer and opponent-history channels remain missing.

The receipt binds 123 projection/serialization/data dependency source files and 14 raw artifact/data files, including the execution plan, claims, results, compressed decision streams, original capture inputs, exact game data and projectile catalog. Native level-reader and runtime attestation hashes are checked for every frame against the recorded producer plan. Concurrent source or input changes invalidate receipt generation.

`clasher.rl.native_public_calibration.verify_calibration_receipt(path)` verifies those bindings and deterministically repeats the saved-frame audit. A JSON boolean alone cannot satisfy this API. The readiness owner can pin the receipt file's hash and require this verifier.

Limits remain explicit in the typed receipt: native known-channel calibration is established for this scope; camera accuracy and measured own-card level calibration are not. Public masks and coordinate transport are verified, but historical v6 streams did not retain per-command scheduling acknowledgements and tick+1 spend snapshots. Execution acceptance is therefore not independently re-audited here. The future native transport recorder/verifier supplies that separate evidence. No historical result is rewritten and no fresh acceptance claim follows from this opened audit.

Local checks: 43 focused tests passed, including raw/projection corruption, level/frame binding, unknown own levels, scope-flag rejection and existing native serialization/refill checks. No native calls, emulator changes, training or gameplay fitting were performed.
