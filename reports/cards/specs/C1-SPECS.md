## 1. Bowler — finite straight boulder, not terminal area explosion

**Symptom.** Level-11 HP 2081 and hit 289 agree. `Bowler/knight`: Knight hits are identical at 30,80,130,180,230,280,330 (last 32 HP), but tower hits are **9 / 2**: ours starts hitting the tower behind him at 74, reference only 475,525. `alone` tower ticks are 164,214,… / 165,215,… (one-tick tolerated difference).

**Root cause.** `entities.py:Troop._create_projectile` builds a `RollingProjectile` whose range starts at the **muzzle**; `RollingProjectile.update` takes a terminal sample and enlarges its radius from `projectileRadius=1000` to `radius=1800` at the endpoint. The raw travel range is also 7500 instead of 7000. RoyaleSim `combat.rs:fire` builds a straight shot whose endpoint is ProjectileRange from the **attacker's centre**, and `step_straight` expires it before the reaching step. `straight_hits` always uses ProjectileRadius=1000, never the unrelated area radius. Those endpoint errors let a Knight-directed boulder hit the remote tower. `RollingProjectile._apply_knockback` additionally uses the shot direction; RoyaleSim `straight_hits` pushes **radially from the current projectile sample**.

**Mechanic vs stat.** Mechanics: origin of the finite range, expiration-before-endpoint, no terminal AOE, radial push. Separate travel-range overlay: BowlerProjectile `projectileRange: 7500 -> 7000` (a direct table difference omitted by the headline balance table); keep damage 113 base/289 at level 11. Do not change movement speed or attack period.

**Python change.** Add an explicit unit straight-shot mode, set only for Bowler's boulder initially. Snapshot body origin O, native-grid muzzle M (1000 ahead), and immutable endpoint A=O+normalized(target−O,7000), rather than M+range. Step toward A by serialized speed170, re-aiming at A each tick with RoyaleSim `path.rs:advance_client`: integer `n=isqrt(dx²+dy²)`, return A when n<=170, else add `trunc(dx*170/n)`, `trunc(dy*170/n)` to the current native point. This differs from the current constant wide-vector rolling step; use no fractional carry. If the proposed step reaches A, expire **without moving or hitting**. Otherwise test a circle of radius 1000 at the new sample, plus target's circular collision radius; one hit per entity ID for the shot. No terminal call at radius 1800. Preserve creation-tick no-step behavior. Use radial knockback from the sample point with push 1000 and existing mass/immunity rules, `interrupts_combat=False`, `reset_hit_on_movement=True`, as the radial branch already supports. Do not change the rectangular rolling-spell implementation.

**Native change.** `projectiles.rs:rolling_character_tick` currently increments `traveled` from the muzzle, samples terminal range, and enlarges `projectile_radius`. Export the mode and body-origin endpoint in `engine-cards/differential.py`/snapshot adapter; mirror the Python step/expiry exactly. `rolling_character_hits` currently constructs an artificial centre behind each victim to create a directional push: use the actual shot point for this mode. Keep the old branch for other rolling characters.

**C56 risk.** Shared rolling paths serve **Log, BarbLog**; straight/piercing collision infrastructure serves **Firecracker, Princess** (plus new-card EliteArcher/Hunter). Recoil/knockback helpers affect nearly every ground troop. Gate by the boulder's new mode, not `pushback>0` globally; no change to C56 shot endpoints, hitbox shape, first sample or snapshot defaults.

**Verify.** `Bowler/knight` must lose all seven spurious tower hits (74 through374) while retaining the exact Knight hit sequence; `alone`/`minions` retain 289 damage and 50-tick firing periods. Add focused last-in-range/beyond-range and off-axis radial-push probes; compare Python/native each tick, including `hit_ids`, push state and next ID. Re-run Log/BarbLog/Princess/Firecracker C56 fixtures unchanged.

## 2. Assassin / Bandit — first-sight anticipation and half-step contact

**Symptom.** HP 906, melee 194, dash 389 agree. `alone`: first dash hit 92/91, death 221/206, seven/six tower hits. `minions`: ours dies at **81**, reference at **94**, with a reference-only 389 tower dash at **91**; ours' first tower event at 174 is a Minion hitting our tower, not a late Bandit hit. `knight` agrees (normal hits 38,58,78,98,118).

**Root cause.** `cards/bandit.py:BanditDash.on_tick` runs before this frame's target selection, starts the charge from last frame's target, then counts a full 800 ms from the following frame. It also rechecks the dash band continuously. `_launch_dash` freezes an exact endpoint at ordinary range from the target; `_update_dash` takes one 500-unit step and hits only upon endpoint arrival. RoyaleSim `state.rs:phase_path16402_for` triggers from **first sight**, marks entry at trigger+15 ticks, holds the **entry** tick still for an untimed dash, then takes **two 250-unit half-steps** and tests ordinary Range+both radii after each. Goal is `dash_goal_native` (a 500-cell centre), and the hit may end travel before the goal. The late entry is decisive against Minions: a fresh current-worktree Python probe reproduced charge at 66 and travel at 82; Assassin is killed at 81 before travel begins. The exact contact point also changes later tower/Minion trajectories.

**Mechanic vs stat.** Mechanics: trigger/entry/contact, target-history blocking, state/clock handoff. Missing immunity-tail metadata is 100/150 ms (`Assassin` override / RoyaleSim 16.402 data): restore 150 through the declarative omitted-field correction for this body, not a global immunity constant. Base damage, speed, hit interval and load agree.

**Python change.** Replace the target-prior-tick charge adapter for Bandit-family units with the following movement-state contract, after current-frame targeting:

1. Remember `dash_seen_target`, `dash_blocked`, `entry_tick`, `goal`, `entry_still`. On first sight of a different target, set `dash_blocked = d² < (3500+own_radius+target_radius)²`. It stays blocked for this target's sight lifetime; walking outward later must not create a dash. The outer trigger is d² <= (6000+target_radius)². Use the plain centre-distance squared for this dash test, not target-selection distance discounts.
2. A walking/idle, unstunned unit with an unblocked target inside the outer trigger stands on that first-sight tick and enters at trigger+`800/50−1` = 15. Trigger/stand is not an ordinary attack windup. If target dies, changes, or an ordinary attack begins, cancel the stand; do not repeatedly cancel solely because the target moves across the initial inner band. Preserve the separate Python non-interrupting Freeze contract in §16.
3. At entry, freeze the goal: point both collision radii short of target centre on the actor-to-target line, integer-truncated; map each coordinate to `floor(point/500)*500+250`. Become non-collidable and immune to damage/effects, clear the walking route, and take **no travel step on entry tick**.
4. Each later movement turn takes at most two 250-unit native dash steps toward the frozen goal. After each, test `range+own_radius+target_radius` against the target's **current point in the sequential movement pass**. If in reach, deal scaled dash damage once and end. A dead target or arrival at goal out of reach ends without damage. Use RoyaleSim `move16402.rs:dash_half_step` arithmetic (see §7), not interpolation.
5. On end: repair a ground endpoint over water using the nearest-land grid; clear dash sight history; seed ordinary load=600, progress=0, unlock the target; advance the next attack from that load on the following combat pass. Preserve 150 ms damage-only tail (`now < end+150`); status/forced-movement immunity ends with travel, retaining Python's Fisherman exception. Do not multiply already-scaled attack damage to derive 389: scale the distinct dash base 152.

**Native change.** Mirror these fields and transitions in `dash.rs:{State,dash_combat,dash_movement}` and `dash_snapshot.py`; export an opt-in fidelity mode for `Assassin`/`BossBandit`. `dash_combat` currently checks/reselects band every charge tick and `dash_movement` steps to `destination` in one whole step; replace only under the new mode. Install the new load/progress state through the ordinary clock reseed facility so `cooldown` projection agrees with Python on landing. Save every nondefault private field needed for a snapshot/resume in the middle of anticipation or either half-step.

**C56 risk.** No C56 deployable card owns `BanditDash`; **Prince, DarkPrince, HogRider, RoyalHogs** share ground movement/jump arithmetic, and **MightyMiner, InfernoTower** share continuous-effect targeting of an immune target. Keep their paths and global targeting policy unchanged. Tower and Minion changes in these mixed scenarios are expected consequences of different Bandit movement, not authorization to alter C56 clocks.

**Verify.** All three Assassin fidelity scenarios, prioritizing Minions: immunity begins in time to survive tick 81 and tower dash hits at 91. `knight` remains equal. Probe first sight below/at min band, a target walking outward afterward, entry tick with a same-tick incoming projectile, target movement before/after dasher, target death and water endpoint. Verify no C56-only digest/snapshot drift.
