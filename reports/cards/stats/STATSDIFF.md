## Kinds

- **MISMATCH**: both sides have a value and they differ.
- **ABSENT**: gamedata has no such field; RoyaleSim has a non-zero value.
- **RS-ABSENT**: RoyaleSim has no such value; gamedata has a non-zero one.
- **UNCERTAIN**: the two sources may not mean the same thing (note says why); treat as a question, not a fix.

## Conventions and assumptions

- Both sides are level-1 base values (RoyaleSim's unified level 1, calibration.json STAT_BASE_LEVEL). Level-1 agreement is visible on the cards that match fully, e.g. Pekka 1469 HP on both sides.
- Distances are millitiles (1000 = 1 tile) on both sides; times are ms; speeds are the raw Supercell speed column on both sides; hit speed -1 means "no repeat" on both.
- Ranged damage: a unit with a projectile has its damage on the projectile (RoyaleSim uses the same rule for damage_source). Our `damage` falls back to `projectileData.damage`.
- Area damage radius: our `areaDamageRadius`, else `projectileData.radius` (splash), compared with RoyaleSim's `area_damage_radius_milli`.
- Attacks air/ground/buildings-only come from our `tidTarget` (TID_TARGETS_*) and `attacksGround`. A block with neither `tidTarget` nor `attacksGround` (for example GoblinCage, Tombstone, BarbarianHut) shows RoyaleSim's `attacks_ground` as ABSENT.
- Counts: our missing `summonNumber` means 1 (RoyaleSim count 1).
- Spawner: our `spawnNumber` / `spawnInterval` / `spawnPauseTime` on the unit block and `spawnCharacterData` as the spawned unit. Spawner start time and spawn radius are not in gamedata.
- Death spawn: our `deathSpawnCount` and `deathSpawnCharacterData` (the unit's own deployTime is compared as "death spawn deploy", flagged UNCERTAIN).
- Spells: damage, radius, duration and hit flags come from the AOE block (projectileData for Snowball; Rage uses its spawnAreaEffectObjectData); Heal's damage and radius come from its HealSpirit projectile.
- Sub-units (death spawns, spawners, morphs, variants, spell summons) are compared on base stats only, with the same rules, against RoyaleSim's unit records of the same name.
- Not compared: load_first_hit (RoyaleSim bool; gamedata has no equivalent key on unit blocks); crown tower damage percent (gamedata and RoyaleSim encode it differently; not in the requested list); mass, stop/wait timers, pushback, charge and jump fields (not in the requested list); spell action graphs (spawn lists inside Graveyard, Vines, Clone, etc.).
- Flying: gamedata has no flying field on unit blocks, so `flying height` is reported as ABSENT where RoyaleSim has a non-zero value.
- gamedata.json's META.canonicalization records deliberate calibration overrides away from the admitted source (Barbarian HP 270 to 280, IceSpirits/FireSpirits/ElectroSpirit HP 90 to 84, Wallbreakers projectile 137 to 110, Goblinstein doctor 36 to 53, Goblin_Stab 47 to 49, Rascals deploy delay). They are not treated as errors here; any that still differ from RoyaleSim appear in the table like any other value.
