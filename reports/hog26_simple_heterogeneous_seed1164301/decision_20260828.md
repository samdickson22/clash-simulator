# Hog 2.6 paired heterogeneous Simple Gym pilot

## Decision

Reject every trained checkpoint and retain
`checkpoints/hog26_u46x2_reactive_slow_spatial_seed1154001/candidate.pt`.

The heterogeneous curriculum fixed the strategy/deck/seat pairing defect and
successfully mixed random, strategy, and exact frozen-parent Hog-mirror rows.
It did not produce a policy that clears the gameplay no-regression gates.
Opponent-mixture tuning alone is no longer the next training move.

## Five-update screen

All 25 checkpoints received four paired games against balanced,
bridge-pressure, and diverse random opponents on seed `1164302`. The parent
update-0 checkpoint scored 3-9. No trained checkpoint exceeded it.

- Updates 5-70 were generally 0-12 to 2-10.
- Update 75 was the only checkpoint to win a bridge-pressure screen game.
- Update 105 tied the parent at 3-9 through a second random win while losing a
  balanced win.
- Updates 75 and 105 were the only finalists escalated.

The Hog-mirror gain does not compensate for random and held-out balanced
regression. Update 105 is rejected without interpolation because the previous
pilot already showed that weight blending merely trades matchup buckets and
does not establish a robust improvement.

## Research consequence

Two independent, numerically stable Simple Gym PPO campaigns have now failed
the same broad promotion requirement under materially different opponent
mixtures. Continuing to tune league weights would be endless patching.

The next bounded experiment should change the learning signal:

1. retain the exact resident simulator, public masks, paired schedules, and
   corrected evaluator;
2. add an on-policy public-information StrategyBot teacher on the learner's
   own states (DAgger-style, not teacher-forced trajectories);
3. supervise play/wait/ability, card slot, and tile with independently
   normalized hierarchical losses so no-op frequency cannot drown out plays;
4. keep PPO/outcome learning as a secondary term rather than asking sparse
   terminal wins plus dense shaping to discover the reactive baseline;
5. stop at small checkpoints and promote only on free-running gameplay.

The balanced public-information StrategyBot is the appropriate first teacher:
the existing exact teacher matrix scored 19-9 across all six strategies plus
random, mean 0.679, and worst-matchup score 0.5—far above every neural policy.
