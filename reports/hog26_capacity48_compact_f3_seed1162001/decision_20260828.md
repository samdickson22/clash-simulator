## Interpretation

The 64-wide compact branch learned the imbalanced offline wait distribution but
did not transfer a usable play decision to free-running states. More epochs,
threshold tuning, or mirror self-play would patch a failed causal mechanism and
are not justified by this evidence. The checkpoint is not promoted and should
not initialize future RL.
