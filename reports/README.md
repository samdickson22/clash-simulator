Many studies refer to frozen runtimes, recordings, checkpoints, and data stored outside Git. Old commands can also contain machine-specific paths. Preserve their provenance when reproducing a result; do not replace missing evidence with new runs under an old receipt. Later studies may supersede earlier recommendations without deleting the earlier outcomes.
