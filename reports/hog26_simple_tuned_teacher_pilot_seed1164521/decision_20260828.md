# Tuned Candidate-17 online-teacher pilot

## Decision

Reject every trained checkpoint and retain
`checkpoints/hog26_u46x2_reactive_slow_spatial_seed1154001/candidate.pt`.

The earlier seed-1164501 failure was confounded because it distilled the
default balanced controller rather than the tuned Candidate-17 controller.
Commit `e8558361` removed that confound: the exact 13 configuration values are
versioned, passed tensor-versus-Python action parity on initial and pressured
states, and are persisted in every checkpoint. Candidate-17 still did not
produce a gameplay improvement.

## Research consequence

Online StrategyBot distillation is rejected as the next policy-improvement
route, not merely retuned again. The retained policy already matches the
teacher's play/wait decision and card slot almost perfectly on visited states;
the auxiliary loss is overwhelmingly exact tile imitation. Optimizing that
teacher-specific tile target changed actions without improving outcomes.

The next experiment must change the target, not the teacher coefficient:

1. use outcome- or value-ranked spatial targets rather than one deterministic
   heuristic tile;
2. preserve the strong parent with a genuinely constrained repair mechanism;
3. gate a tiny counterfactual spatial reranker before any further policy
   fine-tuning;
4. require the reranker to improve multiple free-running buckets before it is
   allowed to generate training targets.
