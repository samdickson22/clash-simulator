**Finding:** the strongest supported cause is a shared placement-rule mismatch, specifically ordinary buildings inheriting the native/public mask's *troop* pocket and bridge expansion. RoyaleSim 0.1.34's installed calibration explicitly ships `placement.BUILDING_TERRITORY = own_half`. Destroying an enemy princess does not open building territory in this simulator. This explains all four OUT_OF_TERRITORY taps without needing a footprint-boundary hypothesis. The fifth tap is on a bridge's closed water boundary, and also outside building territory. Building footprints matter for relocation, but simply eroding every legal tap by the Cannon's 3×3 box would remove simulator-accepted relocations and is not the minimal correction.

Recommend a narrowly scoped, common-arm, public-only mask intersection for ordinary building taps, followed by bounded exact-runtime probes and the unchanged full DEV acceptance gate. Do not patch the running/frozen source or override RoyaleSim's territory rule. This report proposes work; it does not claim the fix or its tests have run.

## Evidence establishing the mismatch

1. **Installed simulator rule, not an inferred real-game rule.** `S/data/calibration.json:1173–1185` states `BUILDING_TERRITORY` is `own_half`, explicitly says the opened band remains refused after an enemy princess falls, and names `state.rs deploy_rule -> Territory::OwnHalf` and `arena.rs box_zone`. Crucially, it labels this rule a LOW-confidence hypothesis about the real client, retained because it is the shipped behavior. Correcting the adapter to this rule means matching this experiment's simulator; it does not establish that real Clash Royale prohibits pocket buildings.

2. **Native/public candidate mask applies troop expansion to ordinary buildings.** `R/src/clasher/rl/public_action_mask.py:83–121` builds the common zone with own rows 1–14/back area, enemy-side rows 17–20 when the corresponding public tower HP is zero, and opened bridge tiles. At :272–291, a conventional building uses `zone & non_blocked`, like a troop, with its footprint then used for visible-body occupancy. At :300–335, the ordinary-building path has no separate own-half territory restriction. `R/src/clasher/rl/contract_v5.py:529–550` only switches to anchored occupancy for enemy-side-capable buildings such as Goblin Drill; it does not correct ordinary Cannon territory.

3. **This Python mask is the actual root-candidate entry point.** `R/src/clasher/rl/c56_rollout_planner.py:73–89` builds `bots['balanced'].mask_builder`, masks scripted/policy proposals, and samples random legal tiles. `W/bot/search/players.py:151–181` separately obtains the policy fallback/proposals and then calls that candidate builder. Therefore changing only a native rollout mask, or filtering only one proposal source, is insufficient. The native parallel rule also opens pockets/bridges (`R/engine-rs/src/scripts.rs:258–274`, :319–335). Native `apply_action` tests the tap's territory and then resolves a static building anchor (`R/engine-rs/src/lib.rs:1134–1183`, :3628–3675). These Rust files are byte-identical to the inspected local game-source files at `/mpac/sdicks02/repos/clasher/engine-rs/src/`.

## Minimal fair, reversible correction to consider after this audit

Introduce one explicit simulator-compatibility mask wrapper in the experimental adapter/factory, with existing masks as its input. For an **ordinary building** (public own card identity; excluding documented enemy-side/tunnelling placement, resolving Mirror through public own-last-play if needed), preserve the existing mask on own-frame tile rows 0–14 and clear that slot's rows 15–31. Equivalently:

`compatible[a] = existing[a] AND (not ordinary_building(own_card_for_slot(a)) OR own_tile_y(a) < 15)`

This is a mask intersection, never an unmask. Existing fences, affordability, public occupancy and other restrictions remain enforced. No per-case IDs, failed-action blacklist, arm, outcome, true opponent level, hidden entity, or future state enters the predicate. The native mask's only extra ordinary-building territory was bridge/pocket territory, so this change directly removes the unsupported category. Do not forbid otherwise legal own-side taps merely because their *unrelocated* 3×3 footprint crosses the river.
