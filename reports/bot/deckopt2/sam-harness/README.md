# Sam deck follow-up harness

This job reuses the original full-pool adapter, copied models, truth simulator and deployed serial player construction. No search algorithm, trained model, frozen runtime or prior scored bank changes.

`sam_bank.py upgrades|max|current` registers independent fresh banks before games. Sam's six upgrade vectors and exact resource budget are frozen in private job JSON inputs. The upgrade bank has60paired cases per R3a/v1/strong policy, six plans and both serial players (2,160games). MiniPekka+Cannon is the reference and pre-approved fallback. The other two banks each have100cases/policy, three specified decks and both players (1,800games each). MAX truth uses16for all cards and towers on both sides; current uses the02:51–02:55Z account snapshot and our King7. No opponent King data exists, so opponent7is an explicit assumption in account-level rows.

`sam_worker.py` runs fresh players per game with persistent per-worker Runtimes, records actual truth levels and passive diagnostics, and retains outcomes. `run-sam.sh LABEL WORKERS` sets exact r3/model/env paths and uses a separate detached shared CPU lease. `coordinate.py` on light05 probes small admissible claims, caps registration at40workers per host and gives upgrade cases first priority. All arms of one case stay on one worker/host, with randomized arm order. Atomic claims are made by `sam_queue.py`; a crashed claim is never automatically retried/released.

Preflights: `level_check.py` is the native-only, model-free fixture validating L16 card/tower HP without a L15 clamp. `max_replay.py`/`run-max-replay.sh` reuse the approved serial construction and repeat an unscored L16 case for identical hashes. Use these entries. The original experimental `sam_worker.py --smoke` fixture is retained for provenance of its failed tick0 deployment and is not the preflight entry. Neither failed fixture entered any scored bank. The existing serial receipt releases priority upgrade work; MAX claims wait for the extra MAX replay receipt.

`report.py` performs20,000 paired within-policy bootstrap resamples with the same case indices for all arms. The strong reserve row ranks upgrades first. A leader must have positive paired lower95%CI against every other plan to be called separated; otherwise choose the approved MiniPekka6→7 then Cannon6→7 fallback. The plan is frozen only at the full registered sample. Sam's deck itself is not selected by these tests. Report both players and separate weak/strong/pooled rows; rext-bc is unavailable for true full-pool decks.

`audit.py upgrades|max|current` requires the complete registered sample, verifies every arm's inputs, truth levels, terminal result and scoped player construction, checks disjoint seeds and preserved raw rows, and writes a final integrity receipt. Its optional `--partial` mode writes a distinct, explicitly incomplete receipt and cannot certify a decision. `diagnostic_metrics.py` computes passive reaction, undefended-share and timing summaries without importing a Runtime or game module. Saturated all-win samples can have degenerate bootstrap intervals; they do not establish equivalence or a100% population win rate.

`refresh_loop.py` is light05-only: it gathers owned controller/error receipts, updates REPORT.md and safely reduces only verified own tail leases after workers fork. `monitor.py` observes bank/hash/count/clean status without mutation. Keep PROGRESS.md and controller receipts for recovery. Opponent refusals are ordinary policy behavior. Our refusals/native/live/nonterminal failures remain flags.
