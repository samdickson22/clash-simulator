# Clasher learning-objective and curriculum audit — 2026-08-17

Scope: read-only audit of the actor/critic objectives, reward profiles, imitation
corpora, PPO, PFSP, deck curriculum, and promotion evidence. No training or
checkpoint mutation was performed. The only workspace change is this report.

## Bottom line

The current research policy is not being held back by a missing reward bonus or
by too few PPO updates. The evidence repeatedly shows that a numerically stable
optimizer can move a policy away from useful gameplay, and that lower imitation
loss can coexist with catastrophic passivity or overplay. The next experiment
should therefore correct a recurrent-corpus causality defect before changing the
reward, model width, or opponent mixture.

The most important decisions are:

1. **Rebuild the accepted three-oracle rehearsal corpus without teacher-forcing
   unexecuted oracle actions into `previous_actions`.** This is a concrete input
   trajectory defect, not a hypothesis.
2. **Keep complete-game outcomes and matched gameplay as the authority.** Use
   imitation and PPO losses only as diagnostics/optimization mechanisms.
3. **Do not start another hand-designed defense coefficient sweep.** The only
   reward experiment still justified is a principled gamma-correct potential
   formulation, followed separately by an elixir-leak ablation.
4. **Keep the privileged critic, but do not use observational state value as a
   Q-function.** Pretraining the critic on complete outcomes is worth testing;
   inference-time state-value reranking has already failed quarantine gates.
5. **Replace static hardest-opponent allocation with a stratified league:** fixed
   safety floors, immutable historical policies, diverse strategy bots, and PFSP
   within—not instead of—those strata.
6. **Retain the 1,720-deck card-balanced procedural pool and frozen evaluation
   pools.** Add matchup/difficulty balancing; do not sequentially train through
   arenas and forget earlier distributions.

## Critical finding: the mixed recurrent corpus has false action history

### Current behavior

`_collect_oracle_shard` in `src/clasher/rl/imitation.py` correctly stores the
action actually executed on the preceding transition in `previous_actions`.
For the slow-push, reactive-defense, and split-lane corpora, the behavior policy
owns the trajectory and the oracle is executed with probability 0.1.

`combine_imitation_corpora` in `src/clasher/rl/imitation_mix.py` then replaces
every non-start `previous_actions[t]` with `expert_actions[t-1]`. This is valid
only when the expert action was actually executed. In these DAgger corpora it
usually was not. The result feeds the recurrent model an oracle action followed
by a board state generated by a different behavior action. `previous_rewards`
remain from the real executed trajectory, making the recurrent context internally
inconsistent as well.

### Direct measurement

The source arrays and combined artifact were read without mutation. For every
non-start row, the stored actual previous action was compared with the previous
oracle label:

| Corpus | Non-start rows | Actual previous action differs from prior oracle | Rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| slow-push oracle | 4,077 | 792 | 19.43% |
| reactive-defense oracle | 4,082 | 794 | 19.45% |
| split-lane oracle | 4,081 | 526 | 12.89% |
| combined `mix_oracle3_12k` | 12,240 | 0 | 0% by forced rewrite |

The three source metadata objects all declare `expert_probability=0.1`. The
combined artifact's zero disagreement is therefore not evidence of a cleaner
trajectory; it is evidence that the source history was overwritten.

### Smallest discriminating A/B

Create a new corpus artifact from the exact same three source files, row order,
episode selection, and split seed, preserving source `previous_actions` and
`previous_rewards` for non-start rows. Only reset context at true episode starts.
Do not overwrite the existing corpus or checkpoint. Fit the same one-epoch,
action-type-head-only candidate from the same initial checkpoint and seed.

Before fitting, add two fail-closed provenance checks:

- DAgger/relabelled corpora must declare whether labels were executed;
- a combiner may synthesize previous expert actions only for an explicitly
  teacher-forced source. Otherwise it must preserve executed history.

Also forbid recurrent deduplication that removes time steps unless an explicit
time-gap feature is retained. The current 12K merge happened to remove zero rows,
but the generic combiner can compress model-owned clock time.

### Advancement and rejection gates

This is a data-correctness experiment, so an offline win alone is insufficient.

- Exact corpus membership, episode boundaries, observations, masks, targets,
  source digests, and sample weights must match the control; only previous-action
  context may differ.
- Evaluate 128 complete disjoint human episodes: action-type NLL/accuracy, play
  recall, played-card slot accuracy, play Brier score and ECE. Report all changes;
  no single metric can promote.
- Replay the same two 24-game six-strategy blocks. Advance only if aggregate
  score is at least the 30-18 control, no opponent loses more than one game over
  its eight combined samples, and playable-no-op does not worsen by more than
  two absolute points.
- Preserve at least 6-2 on the same whole-archetype screen and run a direct
  24-game candidate/control match before replacing the parent.
- Reject immediately on a new frozen workload, any held-out-archetype record
  loss, or a recurrence/calibration regression without a compensating gameplay
  gain.

## Decision registry

### L1. Reward signal

**Current design.** `SelfPlayBattleEnv` emits a zero-sum delta of a clipped
public-state potential plus terminal ±1, an invalid-action penalty, and a
zero-sum elixir-leak penalty. `objective-v1` combines crowns, Princess pressure,
exposure-gated King pressure, tiebreak edge, and early King chip. `defense-v2`
adds board material and tower danger; `defense-v3` strengthens board/danger/King
terms. The accepted structured lineage uses `objective-v1`.

**Evidence.**

- The old defense-v2 update-1420 pilot improved incoming danger and passed its
  first 24-game safety screen, but was not promoted from that sample alone.
- Repeated defense-v2/defense-v3 continuations on later lineages were stable but
  produced no favorable complete-game changes or caused workload regressions.
- Sparse terminal-only `outcome-v1` collapsed toward wait: balanced fell from
  9-3 to 2-10 and playable no-op rose from 0.738 to about 0.98.
- Stateful defense-event reward, procedural defense scenarios, mixed scenario/
  normal PPO, and localized defensive heads improved the synthetic task but
  regressed ordinary games, Hog use, or both.
- Thus “more defense reward” and “terminal only” are both empirically closed as
  coefficient-tuning directions on the tested policies.

**Unresolved mathematical defect.** The environment uses
`Phi(s_next) - Phi(s)` while PPO discounts by `gamma=0.995`. Strict
potential-based shaping requires `gamma * Phi(s_next) - Phi(s)` and an absorbing
terminal potential of zero. The current discounted return therefore contains a
small occupancy preference for keeping the shaped potential high and a terminal
potential residue; it is not policy-invariant shaping.

**Separate unvalidated incentive.** A no-op at full elixir can cost 0.010 before
the step and another 0.005 afterward. At an eight-tick decision interval this can
sum to 1.125 over 30 seconds or 11.25 over a full 300-second match—larger than the
terminal win reward. The tests prove zero-sum arithmetic, not that the coefficient
improves gameplay. It can reward arbitrary spending over strategically correct
waiting.

**Smallest useful reward experiment.** After fixing L0/L1 corpus history, run a
matched three-arm 20-update screen from the same clean BC parent:

1. current objective-v1 delta plus leak penalty (control);
2. gamma-correct objective-v1 shaping, terminal `Phi=0`, leak unchanged;
3. the gamma-correct arm with leak penalty disabled.

Do not add defense-v4 or tune weights in this screen. Keep terminal reward,
optimizer, rollout seeds, league, and transition budget fixed.

**Promotion/rejection.** Advance only a candidate that improves the 216-game
external matrix (random, six strategies, fixed policy anchors) without lowering
any workload score, preserves both seats, does not worsen crown differential,
playable-no-op, full-elixir action calibration, defense-event success, or any of
the 12 designated win-condition utilization rows, and then confirms on frozen
validation and whole-archetype decks. Reject on a >5-point play-rate shift, any
win-to-loss paired regression, or a reward-curve-only “win.”

### L2. PPO and policy preservation

**Current design.** Recurrent PPO uses 64-step truncated BPTT, gamma 0.995, GAE
lambda 0.95, clipped policy and value losses, advantage normalization, gradient
clipping, KL early stopping, optional factorized entropy, parameter L2, rollout
policy KL, recurrent rehearsal, and anchor-rehearsal KL.

**Evidence.** The mechanics are conventional and numerically guarded, but many
stable runs regressed complete play. Examples include update-1500, equal-weight
1440, fresh update-250, low-rate/rolling-anchor/reset-optimizer branches, causal
diverse-league updates, and adapter-only timing. Stable KL/explained variance is
necessary but repeatedly not sufficient.

**Decision.** Keep PPO as the consequence-optimization phase, not as the initial
teacher or the experiment selector. Use short bounded phases from a causally
valid BC/rehearsal parent. Roll back to the last accepted immutable checkpoint
after every failed gate; do not continuously train through a failed endpoint.

**Smallest A/B.** Once the corpus fix is selected, compare the same 20-update
league continuation with and without a moderate frozen-parent action-distribution
KL on both rollout states and a fixed recurrent rehearsal set. Do not compare
parameter L2, policy KL, and rehearsal weights simultaneously.

**Gate.** Require the candidate to beat its parent directly, improve the fixed
external matrix, and retain human replay calibration and held-out decks. Reject
if KL protection merely makes the run behaviorally inert, or if smoother policy
drift does not improve complete outcomes.

### L3. Critic and value targets

**Current design.** The critic has a separate exact simulator encoder and may
receive privileged entities/cards/globals unavailable to the public actor. PPO
fits a clipped MSE value head to GAE returns. Opponent hand and elixir auxiliary
targets supervise actor memory, not the critic encoder. The accepted gate-zero
resource-only update deliberately had `value_coef=0`; its action-type rehearsal
parent froze the value function.

**Evidence.** Privileged value training is causally safe for centralized
training/decentralized execution, and the old PPO lineages often reached high
explained variance. Yet an observational public outcome head with held-out AUC
up to roughly 0.81 under exact zero-sum pairing failed as an action reranker.
Four/eight-step value-guided search and later action-value heads each produced
held-out or quarantine win-to-loss regressions. A state value is not a causal Q
estimate for off-policy root actions.

**Decision.** Retain the separate privileged critic. Do not route critic features,
opponent private state, or value-search choices into the live actor. Do not use
explained variance or AUC as promotion evidence.

**Smallest A/B.** Pretrain only the critic encoder/value head on balanced complete
simulator trajectories with terminal outcome as the primary target and optional
auxiliary crown/tower endpoints. Compare against the same actor with a randomly
initialized/current critic, then give both identical short PPO budgets. Actor
weights and initial action hashes must be exact before PPO.

**Promotion/rejection.** The value arm must improve held-out Brier/AUC in early,
middle, and late game, PPO explained variance, and decisions-to-reach the same
gameplay score. It is retained only if the final paired gameplay gate is no worse.
Reject if it only reduces critic loss, if calibration worsens in any phase, or if
using the head for action overrides causes any quarantine regression.

### L4. Behavior cloning and demonstration data

**Current design.** The fitter supports exact, spatial-v1, type-head-only, and
slot-choice objectives; recurrent complete episodes; left/right augmentation;
clipped card/action-type balancing; parent KL; confidence-aware public inputs;
and trainable-scope restriction. Forced-only mask rows can be excluded. Human TV
Royale type-only labels never train location; strict-v3 labels may.

**Evidence.**

- Broad human imitation created useful warm starts, but no-op imbalance and state
  mismatch repeatedly made lower NLL worse in games.
- Direct degraded-state human imitation collapsed play recall near zero. Exact-to-
  public policy distillation was safe only after an uncertainty gate guaranteed
  exact-state identity.
- Full spatial oracle imitation failed with only 762 placements; action-type-only
  causal repairs produced the recent 19-29 -> 23-25 -> 27-21 -> 30-18 gains.
- A three-epoch consolidated type fit overplayed whenever legal and was rejected;
  the one-epoch, low-rate, parent-KL arm was safer.
- The current HF public-v2 data has 29,722 decision rows but only 4,610 strict
  locations, no status scalars, and no opponent event/history tensors. The newer
  permissioned YouTube source remains outside training until its replacement gate
  passes.

**Decision.** Use causal structured BC/rehearsal as initialization, with explicit
source/task heads or batches rather than pretending human, oracle, and simulator
labels are identically distributed. Keep actor-private fields out; critic-only
labels must be typed separately. Use confidence-weighted losses and whole-episode
splits. Do not mix HF into a future YouTube training corpus once YouTube passes
the predeclared replacement gate; preserve HF only as immutable out-of-domain
evaluation.

**Smallest A/B after causal-history repair.** On the same future YouTube split,
compare current clipped joint sample weighting against episode-balanced batches
with separate bounded quotas for wait/play, archetype, and played card. Hold total
optimizer examples fixed and retain proper calibration metrics. Do not increase
epochs until this sampling A/B passes gameplay.

**Gate.** Report source-specific and aggregate NLL, Brier/ECE, play recall, wait
recall, conditional card accuracy, and spatial distance by mechanic. Promotion
still requires the two frozen strategy blocks, direct parent games, held-out
deck/archetype games, and per-win-condition utilization. Reject any arm whose
aggregate accuracy improves by predicting more no-ops, whose play recall rises by
unconditional overplay, or whose source-specific metric regresses materially.

### L5. Spatial imitation objective

**Current design.** The model already factorizes
`P(slot, tile)=P(slot/type)*P(tile|slot)`. Exact 2,306-way CE is algebraically
the same as exact type NLL plus exact conditional-location NLL. `spatial-v1`
adds conservative probability mass to legal neighboring tiles for troops and
area spells, while buildings/rolling spells stay exact.

**Evidence.** Splitting exact CE alone adds no information. Existing playable
accuracy shows card/type can improve while exact location remains very low.
Current public-v2 type corpora use tile zero as a placeholder and must never train
location. Strict data has far fewer rows and many underrepresented cards.

**Decision.** Keep type and location metrics/objectives separate. Do not train a
global spatial head on type-only rows or infer spatial quality from joint NLL.
Use `spatial-v1` only after the new source clears strict location provenance and
has sufficient mechanic/card coverage.

**Smallest A/B.** Exact-location CE versus spatial-v1 from identical weights,
strict-v3 rows, seed, batches, and epochs. Predeclare per-mechanic minimum sample
counts; underpowered card classes are “insufficient,” not passes.

**Gate.** Zero-smoothing logits/gradients must match exact CE. Illegal tiles get
zero target mass. Evaluate exact, within-one, within-two, mechanic-tolerant,
correct-card distance and calibration on replay/deck/chronology-disjoint sets,
then complete games. Reject smoothing that trades building/rolling precision or
card selection for a lower location loss.

### L6. Opponent league and PFSP

**Current design.** The trainer can mix random, six deterministic public strategy
bots, and frozen checkpoints across worker slots. Strategy PFSP is currently a
static report-to-worker allocation using `(1-score_rate)^power` plus a floor. It
prioritizes the hardest strategies but does not dynamically rate policy snapshots,
does not target informative near-50% matchups, and cannot prevent an impossible
opponent from consuming excess mass. Historical checkpoint allocation is manual.

**Evidence.** Stationary random and frozen-historical phases produced large gains
in the original lineage. Mixed leagues later improved update 1400, then continued
training regressed into passivity. Equal-weight and safety-heavy variants did not
solve this. The structured parent’s broad league PPO was stable but lost games;
small targeted oracle rehearsal did better. This argues for coverage floors and
short acceptance cycles, not one long adaptive run.

**Decision.** Use a stratified league:

- 25% immutable safety anchors (accepted parent, older distinct policies);
- 25% deterministic strategies with a minimum slot per family;
- 25% informative policy opponents near 40-60% score;
- 15% current/self-play or recent snapshots;
- 10% random/exploration floor.

Apply PFSP within strata and refresh only from a separate tuning deck pool. Save
immutable snapshots at accepted gates, not every noisy update. Treat those
percentages as the first bounded candidate, not doctrine.

**Smallest A/B.** Current static hardest-strategy allocation versus the stratified
allocation above, same clean parent, 20 updates, transitions, actor seats, deck
draws, optimizer, and seeds. Evaluate at update 10 and 20; stop after the first
confirmed regression.

**Gate.** Candidate must improve mean and worst-opponent score, directly tie/beat
its parent, retain safety anchors and random, avoid seat skew, and pass held-out
decks. Reject if it learns a strategy-bot dialect while human replay calibration,
unseen archetypes, or win-condition use regresses.

### L7. Deck and matchup curriculum

**Current design.** The v3 generator created 2,652 structurally valid semantic
variants. After exact frozen-pool exclusions, training has 1,720 decks across
eight archetypes and 62 cards. Sampling preserves equal archetype mass and reduced
card exposure max/min from 23.51x to 5.83x. Four whole archetypes—Graveyard,
Lava Hound, Royal Hogs, X-Bow—remain unseen. PFSP tuning uses a separate 308-deck
pool. Exact validation/held-out signatures are frozen.

**Evidence.** This is substantially stronger than the old small deck rotation and
already enforces deck-level isolation. However semantic substitution validity is
heuristic, equal archetype/card exposure does not balance matchup difficulty, and
an archetype label may hide structurally different control/cycle/beatdown variants.
The frozen eight-game held-out screen is useful as a tripwire but too small for a
skill claim.

**Decision.** Retain the pool and frozen splits. Add a matchup table indexed by
learner archetype, opponent archetype, designated win condition, average elixir,
and measured parent score. Sample coverage first, then difficulty. Never train on
the four frozen archetypes merely to improve their promotion score; create a new
quarantine set before any later decision to expand training coverage.

Do not use a one-way low-arena-to-high-arena curriculum as the primary schedule.
Lower arenas can be a short warm-start stratum, but sequential removal changes the
card/mechanic distribution and invites forgetting. Prefer competency-based mixing:
retain simple matchups while gradually increasing hard/meta matchup mass.

**Smallest A/B.** Current archetype/card-balanced deck sampling versus a bounded
matchup-balanced sampler with the same marginal deck and card exposure. Same
league, transitions, and seed. The candidate advances only if underrepresented
matchups improve without moving aggregate exposure outside the current bounds.

**Gate.** Report every archetype-by-archetype cell, per-card and per-win-condition
utilization, score and crown margin, seat balance, and diversity. Require no frozen
validation/held-out signature overlap and no worst-cell regression. Expand the
held-out evaluation from eight games to multiple decks and both seats before any
human-level claim.

### L8. Promotion and rollback authority

**Current design.** The repository has unusually strong paired-seat comparison,
fixed deck/seed blocks, crown and behavior diagnostics, checkpoint hashes, strict
no-regression utilities, and held-out deck pools. It has also accumulated many
small screens and post-hoc localized repairs.

**Decision.** Use a two-stage rule:

1. **Development advance:** fixed 48-game strategy roster, 24-game direct parent,
   recurrent human replay, eight-game held-out tripwire, utilization/passivity.
2. **Promotion:** at least the established 216-game external matrix plus expanded
   multi-deck whole-archetype and fresh quarantine blocks, all seat-swapped.

Every experiment must bind checkpoint, source corpora, deck pools, evaluator,
seeds, and raw game records by digest. Select only on development data; promotion
and quarantine are one-shot. If a candidate fails, return to the immutable parent
rather than tuning on the failed promotion records.

Training reward, offline loss, exact-action accuracy, explained variance, AUC,
action rate, or one strategy record can never independently promote a policy.

## Ranked roadmap

1. **Causal corpus repair (highest priority, lowest cost):** preserve executed
   previous action/reward histories; matched one-epoch action-type A/B.
2. **New-video extraction contract:** finish YouTube safety/replacement canary;
   retain actor/critic privacy typing, exact play events, strict placements, HP,
   opponent public events, and whole-match split coupling.
3. **Clean supervised initialization:** episode/source-balanced causal rehearsal
   with the explicit type/location provenance rules and proper calibration gates.
4. **Principled reward screen:** current versus gamma-correct potential versus
   gamma-correct/no-leak; no new handcrafted terms.
5. **Critic outcome pretraining:** frozen actor, complete-trajectory targets,
   identical bounded PPO follow-up.
6. **Stratified PFSP pilot:** coverage floors plus informative historical opponents,
   checked at 10/20 updates.
7. **Matchup-balanced deck sampling:** preserve current marginal card/archetype
   exposure and frozen whole-archetype quarantine.
8. **Only after the above:** longer multi-seed RL. Budget scales after a pipeline
   wins the external matrix, not before.

## Rejected or deprioritized directions

- another defense-v2/v3 coefficient sweep;
- terminal-only PPO from the current parent;
- scenario-only or defense-heavy shared PPO;
- more epochs on a causally inconsistent or type-only-spatial corpus;
- observational value used directly as action value;
- static hardest-only PFSP for a long run;
- sequential arena curriculum that drops earlier distributions;
- promotion from optimizer stability, offline loss, a single 12-game screen, or
  win-condition activity without outcomes.
