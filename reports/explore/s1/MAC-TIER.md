# S1 Mac tier note — pending study

No Mac access, deployment or strength admission. S1 will test one-core R3a
coarse-first search with a cached proposer and fallback on the same plain-v1,
honest-lateness scale as K2, at 200 and 160 ms. Results are pending.

The earlier R3a 24.2% loss result used a search opponent and uncharged lateness;
it does not establish parity with K2's 25% or a supported Mac compute tier.
R3a remains killed under its original Stage 1 gates and NEVER-ADOPTABLE there.

The frozen S1 viability rule requires the upper paired 95% CI for S-200 minus
K0c-200 to be at most −10 pp, and S-200 minus K2-200 to be at most +5 pp.
A passing fleet result would support that tested compute tier only. A 160 ms
budget emulates nominal 0.8× speed, with 152/192 = 0.792 scoring-budget ratio;
it does not measure actual Mac throughput.

Loaded Mac admission would still require the existing frame-to-submission,
FPS, packet gap, command acceptance and integration gates, the full perception
and emulator load, the actual four-belief-root pipeline, a separately pinned
arm64 native build, exact score/posterior/RNG equality, charged inference,
all overruns and return tails, and timestamped GC maintenance/poll overlap.
Fleet GC deferral alone does not establish zero delayed live polls.
