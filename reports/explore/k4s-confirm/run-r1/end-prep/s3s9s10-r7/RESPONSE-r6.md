## Deviation (disclosed)

The brief asked for `git symbolic-ref -q <ref>`. `symbolic-ref` is not in the pinned `safe_input.GIT_SUBCOMMANDS` allowlist and safe_input must not change, so both programs ask the same question through the allowlisted, gated `for-each-ref --format=%(refname) %(symref) <ref>` (git 2.25.1 prints the symbolic target; nothing for an absent or dangling ref, where the guard's content check refuses the `ref:` file).
