# Hog 2.6 Simple Gym asymmetric PFSP pilot

## Decision

Reject the complete update-20 policy and all three bounded parent/update-20
weight interpolations. Keep
`checkpoints/hog26_u46x2_reactive_slow_spatial_seed1154001/candidate.pt`
as the retained champion.

The pilot found real, repeatable gains against a random opponent, in the Hog
mirror, and in some bridge/split-lane matchups. It did not produce a policy
that satisfies the held-out balanced no-regression gate. The final 75% blend
was only 5 wins better than the parent over all 192 final-gate games, with 34
paired improvements and 29 paired regressions (`p=0.615` by a two-sided exact
sign test). That is neither a decisive global gain nor a safety-preserving
promotion.

## Stability

The trainer completed updates 1-120 and 61,440 learner transitions without a
fallback or nonfinite checkpoint tensor.

- Mean throughput: 181.49 learner transitions/s (range 166.5-182.4)
- Approximate PPO KL absolute maximum: 0.00041
- Clip-fraction maximum: 0.001
- Anchor-policy KL mean/maximum: 0.00244/0.01661 (maximum at update 57)
- Terminal episodes observed during training: 89
- Training episode record: 7 wins, 82 losses, 0 draws
- Mean play rate: 0.1198
- Mean no-op-when-playable rate: 0.0259

The run was numerically stable. Numerical stability does not imply gameplay
promotion: later checkpoints lost the early policy's limited gains.

## Checkpoint screen

The initial 12-game screen used four paired games each against balanced,
bridge-pressure, and diverse random opponents.

| Checkpoint | Screen record |
| --- | ---: |
| update 20 | 6-6 |
| update 40 | 6-6 |
| update 60 | 6-6 |
| update 80 | 6-6 |
| update 100 | 4-8 |
| update 120 | 5-7 |

Updates 100 and 120 were rejected at the stop gate. Updates 20/40/60/80 were
escalated to the exact 72-game paired strategy matrix. Their records were
13-59, 9-63, 10-62, and 10-62, respectively. Only update 20 remained a
candidate; the current-source parent rerun was 12-60.

## Direct update-20 evidence

Update 20 improved the two retention controls:

| Gate | Parent | Update 20 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Diverse random, 24 games | 7-17 | 11-13 |
| Hog mirror, 24 games | 13-11 | 19-5 |
| Six-strategy matrix, 72 games | 12-60 | 13-59 |

However, across 72 held-out balanced games (Graveyard, Lava Hound, Royal Hogs,
and X-Bow deck families), update 20 fell from 38-34 to 28-44. Paired outcomes
were 6 improvements, 16 regressions, and 50 unchanged; mean crown differential
worsened by 0.292/game. Direct promotion was therefore rejected.

## Bounded interpolation gate

Interpolations at alpha 0.25, 0.50, and 0.75 were produced between the
behavior-identical rebased parent and update 20. On the first 24 held-out
balanced games, their records were 10-14, 11-13, and 14-10. Only alpha 0.75
matched the parent's 14-10 and was escalated.

Final alpha-0.75 results:

| Gate | Parent | Alpha 0.75 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Six-strategy matrix, 72 games | 12-60 | 16-56 |
| Diverse random, 24 games | 7-17 | 7-17 |
| Hog mirror, 24 games | 13-11 | 19-5 |
| Held-out balanced, 72 games | 38-34 | 33-39 |
| **All final gates, 192 games** | **70-122** | **75-117** |

All 192 paired comparisons had identical matchup seed, candidate seat,
candidate deck, and opponent deck. The final paired tally was 34 improved,
29 worsened, and 129 unchanged. The aggregate crown-differential change was
only +0.042/game. The held-out balanced regression remained 11 improved versus
16 worsened with -0.111 crown/game.

## Consequence

Do not continue from update 20, update 120, or the alpha-0.75 blend. Preserve
the learner-only CUDA Graph collector and corrected paired evaluator; those
are successful infrastructure. Replace the opponent curriculum before the next
pilot:

1. train with an explicitly stratified mixture of strategy, frozen-parent, and
   random opponents instead of strategy-only PFSP;
2. report outcomes and sampling counts per opponent type and deck archetype;
3. checkpoint more frequently during the first 20 updates, where all useful
   movement occurred;
4. keep held-out balanced archetypes out of training and use them as an
   automatic rollback gate;
5. require simultaneous non-regression on diverse random, Hog mirror, hard
   strategy, and held-out archetype blocks before promotion.

This is a failed policy promotion but a successful experimental result: the
new backend can train quickly, and the evidence identifies opponent-mixture
imbalance rather than numerical instability as the next problem.
